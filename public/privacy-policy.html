Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 31 August 2026

ParentAI is used by a parent to teach their own child. That shapes everything below: the account is yours, the child is a profile inside it, and we hold as little about them as the product can function on.

Your child has no account with us. No login, no password, no email address, no phone number — nothing they could be contacted through, by us or by anyone else. They cannot sign in, because there is nothing for them to sign in to.

What we collect

About What Why
You Name, email address, optional phone number, password (stored only as a strong hash) To create your account and let you sign in
Your child First name, date of birth, grade, board, school medium, subjects To match lessons to their actual syllabus and pitch them at the right age
Learning Plans generated, sessions run, answers given, scores, streaks To show you whether it is working, and to build the revision phase from what they got wrong
Photographs Textbook pages or worksheets you choose to photograph To ground the lesson in the page in front of you
Household Home language, study window, notification preferences, consent record To write in your language and message you when you asked to be messaged
Devices Device name and type for each signed-in session So you can see where you are signed in, and sign a device out

We do not collect location, contacts, your camera roll, browsing activity, or any advertising identifier. The Android build blocks those permissions outright, and the build fails if one ever reappears.

Who else sees it

Three processors, each doing one job:

  • Google (Gemini) — the topic and any photographed page are sent to generate the lesson. Your name and email are never sent. Google does not use this content to train its models under the paid API terms we use.
  • Cloudinary — stores photographs you upload, on private authenticated URLs that cannot be guessed or shared.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores the database itself, encrypted at rest.

We do not sell your data. We do not show third-party advertising. We do not share anything with data brokers. Marketing email is off unless you switch it on.

Our own staff

Support staff can see that a household exists, what plan it is on, how many sessions have run, and whether lesson generation is failing. They cannot see a lesson's content, a teaching script, or a single answer your child gave — the system does not expose it to them at all. They cannot see or reset your password, and they cannot sign in as you.

Your rights

From Settings → Data and privacy, without asking anyone:

  • Export — everything we hold, as one file
  • Delete — the whole household, permanently, including the photographs. See how to delete your account
  • Correct — edit any detail about you or your child at any time
  • Withdraw consent — deleting the household withdraws it entirely

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete it, the data goes immediately; encrypted infrastructure backups may hold a copy for up to 30 days before ageing out. An account left untouched for 24 months is deleted after we email the address on it.

Children

ParentAI is for use by a parent or guardian. Accounts are held by adults, and you confirm you are the parent or guardian of every child you add. Because a child cannot sign in, cannot message anyone, and is never contacted by us, there is no direct collection from a child at all.

If you believe a child's information reached us without a parent's consent, email us and we will delete it.

Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Nobody, including us, can read them
  • Every connection is encrypted in transit; the database is encrypted at rest
  • Sign-in sessions are per device and can be revoked from your account screen
  • Photographs are stored privately, never on a public or guessable URL

Where your data is

Servers in Singapore, the closest region to India, chosen so lessons arrive quickly. Google's Gemini processes requests in its own regions.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, you will be told in the app and asked to accept the new version. The version you accepted, and when, is recorded on your household.
