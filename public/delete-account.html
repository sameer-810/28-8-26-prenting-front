Delete your ParentAI account

Last updated: 31 August 2026

You can delete your household and everything in it yourself, from inside the app. You do not need to ask us, and we cannot do it for you — the erasure right is yours to exercise.

From the app or the web

  1. Open ParentAI and sign in
  2. Go to Settings → Data and privacy
  3. Tap Delete everything
  4. Type your household name exactly, to confirm
  5. Tap Delete permanently
This cannot be undone. If you want a copy of anything first, use Export everything on the same screen — it downloads a single file containing all of it.

Only the household owner can delete a household. If you were added as a second parent, ask the owner, or delete your own parent profile from Settings → Account.

What is deleted

All of it, immediately, from the live database:

Data What happens
Your parent account — name, email, phone Deleted
Your children's profiles — name, grade, board, school medium Deleted
Every study plan, session, answer and score Deleted
Photographs of textbook pages and worksheets you uploaded Deleted from our image storage, not merely unlinked
Progress history, streaks, mastery records and reports Deleted
Your consent record and notification preferences Deleted
Signed-in devices All signed out, before anything else is removed

How long it takes

The deletion happens as you confirm it — there is no queue and no waiting period. Encrypted infrastructure backups may retain a copy for up to 30 days before they age out; those backups are not readable by our staff and are never restored selectively.

What is kept, and why

Nothing that identifies you or your child. We retain anonymous, aggregated counts — for example "how many sessions ran on 12 August" — which contain no name, no email and no link back to any household. If there is an unresolved payment dispute, the payment record itself is retained for as long as the law requires, and it holds no learning data.

Deleting one child rather than the whole household

Settings → Children, then remove the child. Their profile and their learning history go; your account and any other children stay.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Uninstalling does not delete anything from our side. Sign in at parentai.app in a browser and follow the steps above, or email us from the address on the account and we will guide you through it.
